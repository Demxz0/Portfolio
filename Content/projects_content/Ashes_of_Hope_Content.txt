Overview
Ashes of Hope is a visual novel made in 10 days for SheJam, my first game jam, where it won the Cultural Impact award. Team of 3: me, Leen (developer, also from Jordan), and a Turkish artist we met through the jam's Discord. The jam theme was "Start from Zero. Rebuild. Reimagine. Rise." Our story followed a post-war family that had lost everything but kept finding ways to rebuild and hope again. The original idea was about the war in Gaza, but we had to reshape it to fit the competition's policy and the artist's style.

My Role and Responsibilities
I built the game's core systems: the dialogue logic, the scene structure, and the settings/pause menu. Leen handled art integration, mini-games, and polish. I also wrote the dialogue and came up with the game's title.

Tools
Godot, GDScript, JSON (for dialogue data), Git/GitHub, Gemini (AI assistant), Discord.

Time
10 days total, but real development only started around day 6 — we lost the first days to procrastination and not having a clear plan.

Team Collab
Met the team through Discord for the jam. Leen and I split the dev work; the artist handled all visuals. Coordinating through Git was rough since I was still new to branching and merging, so I ran into conflicts merging my work with Leen's.

Process
I started by writing the core script system: how dialogue moves letter by letter, and how it pulls from JSON files through a global config_handler script that also controlled the settings and pause menu. I split music and sound effects using audio buses. Once the dialogue logic worked on one level, I built out the rest of the levels and the transitions between them.

Breakdown

Dialogue system: JSON-driven, letter-by-letter text
Global ConfigHandler script: settings, pause menu, dialogue control
Audio: separate buses for SFX and music
Levels: built one first to test the system, then the rest

Challenges and Problems Solved

Git merge conflicts from being new to branching — pushed me to actually learn how branches and merges work
Time pressure meant no time left for transition animations or a fully polished ending scene

My Impact
I shaped the actual story and wrote all the dialogue based on what the team agreed on, and I came up with the title.

Decisions
Reworked the original war-focused concept into a story about resilience and rebuilding, to fit the competition's rules and the art style.

How I Used AI
Used Gemini throughout as a teaching assistant, not just to generate code — described the project and it helped me design the dialogue logic and the global script architecture. Said it improved both the system design and how fast I learned it.

Lessons Learned

Manage time better, avoid losing early days
Learned dialogue and settings systems in games
Learned Git branches/merges
Learned how to convey ideas indirectly through a story
Improved teamwork/communication
Improved AI + Godot skills
Learned how to narrate a story through a game
Learned how to tie a jam's theme into a concept
