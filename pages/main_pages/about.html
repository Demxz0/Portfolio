About Me

Who I am

What is all about me?

I close my eyes, picture, wire, solve.

My name is Dima Al-Jundi.

I'm a software engineer with a deep love for games. I don't just play them, I obsess over how they're built. My background in software engineering gives me a strong foundation in code quality: I think in terms of code smells, test cases, refactoring, design patterns, and maintainable systems, and I bring that same rigor into game development.

What I Do

  • Work across both Godot (GDScript) and Unity (C#), with Godot as my engine of choice.
  • Have touched nearly every discipline in indie game development, but I'm strongest and most passionate about gameplay programming and SFX design/manipulation.
  • Participated in 4 game jams — won 1st place in one, and an award in another.

How I Work

  • Analytical by nature; I enjoy breaking down problems, structuring systems, and organizing complexity into something manageable.
  • Natural leader; comfortable dividing tasks, reading people, managing time, and taking responsibility for outcomes.
  • Stay calm under pressure; when something breaks unexpectedly, I focus on fixing it rather than panicking.
  • Quiet, but talk non-stop once I'm on a topic I know well.
  • I gamify almost everything, I've even turned my own life into a gamified system in Notion to manage habits and goals.

My Process

  • I write quick thoughts into Notepad first so nothing gets lost, then get refined into Google Docs when they need real structure (sometimes with AI help to polish the writing).
  • I trust my own imagination and game design knowledge over AI for ideation and GDD writing.
  • I write and architect my own code so I stay in control of the engineering decisions, but I'll use AI to speed up repetitive or well-understood implementation work.
  • In Problem-solving & Debugging I let AI take a first pass on common problems, but if it's struggling, I step in and solve it myself.
