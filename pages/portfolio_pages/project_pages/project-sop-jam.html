Sop Game Jam 2026

Godot3DSoloGame Jam

Overview

This was my first solo game jam — Sop Game Jam 2026, theme "the critical point," 5 days, built in Godot 3D. Inspired by Buckshot Roulette, the concept: figure out if the person across from you is human or AI by pushing them to their breaking point.

My Role and Responsibilities

Solo project — everything: design, systems, dialogue, sound.

Process & Breakdown

I kept the whole game in a single scene (a room, character, and table) on purpose, so I wouldn't burn time building environments and could focus entirely on the core idea. Started with minimal left/right camera movement, then built interactable item logic and character dialogue.

  • A paper prop gives hints indirectly instead of stating rules outright — the player has to work it out
  • Core loop: ask emotional or logical questions within a 90-second timer to push the subject to their critical point
  • Breaking an AI needs Emotional → Logical → Emotional → Logical (confuses it)
  • Breaking a human needs Emotional → Emotional → Logical → Logical (anger builds, then a sudden shift catches them off guard)
  • A monitor shows heartbeat and temperature as a secondary read — though an AI subject can manipulate that too
  • After the timer: give a flower (human) or shoot (AI) — right call gives a true ending, wrong call gives a bad ending

Challenges and Problems Solved

  • Didn't document much early on, which let ideas blur together and contradict each other
  • The game manager ended up as a long chain of if/else statements — recognized afterward that this wasn't a good way to structure it
  • Scope crept steadily even though the starting idea was simple, which ate into time meant for testing

Decisions

Deliberately built as one scene to protect time for the core mechanic instead of environment art — a good call given the 5-day limit, even though the mechanic itself still grew bigger than planned.

Gameplay footage — interrogation scene

Lessons Learned

  • Recognized if/else sprawl in a game manager as a bad pattern to avoid next time
  • Scope creep can happen even after deliberately keeping the concept simple — watch it constantly
  • Skipping documentation early causes ideas to blur and contradict each other later
  • Ran out of time for real playtesting and balance because design/rule-writing took longer than expected
