Sop Jam 2026

Godot 3D Solo

Overview

This was my first solo game jam — Sop Game Jam 2026, theme "the critical point," 5 days, built in Godot 3D. Inspired by Buckshot Roulette, the concept: figure out if the person across from you is human or AI by pushing them to their breaking point.

My Role and Responsibilities

Solo project — everything: design, systems, dialogue, sound.

Process & Breakdown

I kept the whole game in a single scene on purpose, so I wouldn't burn time building environments and could focus entirely on the core idea.

  • Core loop: ask emotional or logical questions within a 90-second timer.
  • Breaking an AI needs: Emotional → Logical → Emotional → Logical.
  • Breaking a human needs: Emotional → Emotional → Logical → Logical.
  • After the timer: give a flower (human) or shoot (AI) — true ending or bad ending.

Challenges and Problems Solved

  • Didn't document much early on, which let ideas blur together.
  • The game manager ended up as a long chain of if/else statements — recognized this wasn't a good way to structure it.
  • Scope crept steadily even though the starting idea was simple.

My Impact & Decisions

I achieved a fully working interrogation-logic system with two distinct correct sequences, an unreliable-narrator dialogue layer, and a working true/bad ending split in just 5 days by scoping the environment down to almost nothing.

Lessons Learned

  • Recognized if/else sprawl in a game manager as a bad pattern.
  • Scope creep can happen even after deliberately keeping the concept simple.
  • Skipping documentation early causes ideas to blur.
  • Design/rule-writing takes a lot of time, leaving less for testing.
