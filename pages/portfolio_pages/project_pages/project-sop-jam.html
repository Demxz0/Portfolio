Sop Game Jam 2026

Godot3DSoloGame Jam

Overview

This was my first solo game jam (Sop Game Jam 2026, theme "the critical point") 5 days, built in Godot 3D. Inspired by Buckshot Roulette, the concept: figure out if the person across from you is human or AI by pushing them to their breaking point.

My Role and Responsibilities

Everything: design, systems, dialogue, sound.

Process & Breakdown

I kept the whole game in a single scene (a room, character, and table) on purpose, so I wouldn't burn time building environments and could focus entirely on the core idea. Started with minimal left/right camera movement, then built interactable item logic and character dialogue.

Items on table are:

  • A paper prop gives hints indirectly instead of stating rules outright, the player has to work it out.
  • Detector: ask emotional or logical questions within a 90-second timer to push the subject to their critical point and know whether they are human or AI.

Challenges and Problems Solved

  • Didn't document much early on, which let ideas blur together and contradict each other.
  • The game manager ended up as a long chain of if/else statements , this game has a lot of conditions so it needs better architecture.
  • Scope crept even though the starting idea was simple, which ate into time meant for testing.
  • I tried to make the character hold a gun and make shoot animation , but failed at making it hold the gun right in right direction in run time even though it is working in editor. I cancelled the idea to focus on more important things since i did not have much time.

Decisions

Deliberately built as one scene to protect time, a good call given the 5-day limit, even though the mechanic itself still grew bigger than planned.

Lessons Learned

  • Game manager should be written carefully in games where there are multiple conditions.
  • Scope creep can happen even after deliberately keeping the concept simple, watch it constantly.
  • Skipping documentation early causes ideas to blur and contradict each other later.
  • Games that needs complex design balancing needs also lots of playtesting so avoid it in solo, tight timeline jams.
