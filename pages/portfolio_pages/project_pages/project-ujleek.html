Ujleek

Unity Puzzle React Web Team Lead Graduation Project

Overview

Ujleek is an educational web platform about common mental disorders for Gen Z, built as my graduation project with a team of 5 over 2.5 months. It combines a written platform with an interactive web game, aimed at making mental health information short, easy to digest, and Gen-Z-friendly.

The Game

Design

A 3D low-poly prototype focused on Anxiety disorder alone , scoped down from an original idea of one location per disorder. Set entirely in a house; using a house specifically pushes back on a common misconception in the Middle East that these disorders are situational rather than something you carry everywhere and for anxiety most unexpectable place is at home.

3 core systems: Anixty meter , breathing system, and a limited medicine system, using one reduce anixty but slows movement and blurs vision, signaling that it helps but has a cost.

Color Theory

Orange represents anxiety (referencing Inside Out 2), used across assets and lighting that darkens as anxiety rises. The final room, once you've resolved all three, shifts to blue (orange's opposite) for comfort and closure.

Three Rooms

  • Attachment Anxiety: a phone call guides a puzzle, then cuts off; anxiety rises to a panic attack unless you find the item the caller pointed at
  • Social Anxiety: judging voices in a dark room; hiding in the bathroom backfires; finding a missing light-switch piece lights the room and reveals no one was ever there
  • Agoraphobia: locked in a kitchen, vision narrowing; the only way out is to stay still and breathe correctly

Challenges

  • First time building a full 3D scene , i oversized the house which made real-time lights barely visible; so i spent lot of time before i diagnosed the issue; by turning a light red and moving it to the ground to isolate the scale issue, then shrinking the house
  • Started game dev in early April, ~6 weeks before deadline, while still running the rest of the project
  • Recorded real voice acting from myself and people I know instead of AI voices , scoping a project needing this much voice acting was a mistake
  • When i was rotating any object (3D model) the model scale becomes ununiformed , an artist a know told me that the problem might be that i placed this object inside a nested objects , so i moved them to the main scene.
  • No optimization knowledge with 1 week left: WebGL export crashed within minutes, baking lights turned some objects black , ultimately i dropped the browser/WebGL goal entirely

Lessons Learned

  • For browser-based games, go 2D , real-time 3D lighting causes major web-export problems and forces you to cut ideas.
  • Don't scope voice-heavy content without a realistic plan for recording it.
  • Keep floating text and narration consistent , mismatched messaging confuses players.
  • AI-generated code architecture needs active correction and supervision. So plan code architecture early, small late-stage fixes cascading into new bugs is a sign it's too fragile.
